What’s in the open data · field guide

Not the study app. A map of the material so you can feel what exists before deciding what belongs on the surface. Expand a layer, then poke the Gen 1:1 sample at the bottom.

jacks-cross/the-concor/docs/data-field-guide.html

Hard problem stated cleanly: the data feels valuable, but until you’ve touched the fields, you can’t know what deserves a ticket, a drawer, or silence. This page is that touch — inventory first, surface later.

Packages (two repos, one family)

morphhb Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible The verse as tokens · WLC text · lemma pointers · morph · stable ids

Source of every word-in-context. Public domain WLC text; lemma/morph CC BY 4.0.

Lives as OSIS XML per book (wlc/Gen.xml etc.), or JSON converters that spit [ wordString, lemma, morphology ] arrays.

Usually this is the clay of the study surface — not optional background.
lexicon Open Scriptures Hebrew Lexicon Strong dictionary · BDB outline · bridges · augmented index

Not the verse. The drawers you open when a lemma number needs a face and a gloss.

Files: HebrewStrong.xml, BrownDriverBriggs.xml, LexicalIndex.xml, AugIndex.xml.

Usually peek / ledger — not the primary ticket text.

Fields you can actually use

morphhb Surface form (as written) The Hebrew string in this verse, with pointing

Contents of the <w> tag. This is “how it was written here,” not the dictionary headword.

בְּ/רֵאשִׁ֖ית · וַ/יֹּ֥אמֶר · הָ/אָֽרֶץ
Default ticket face. Your function-reading hangs off this (or off segments below).
morphhb Segment slash / Prefix / body (and sometimes more) inside one written word

MorphHB often splits attached bits with / in the surface string. That matches multi-part lemmas like b/7225 or c/d/776.

surface: הַ/שָּׁמַ֖יִם
lemma: d/8064 → article + “heavens”
Huge for letter-function study: the “word” is already a small stack of programs. You might want segments as sub-tickets — or ignore the slash and treat the blob as one.
morphhb Lemma (Strong-like pointer) Which dictionary entry(ies) this occurrence claims

Attribute lemma. Can be a bare number, a number + letter (1254 a), or a path of prefixes + numbers (b/7225, c/l/2822).

lemma="b/7225" · lemma="1254 a" · lemma="c/d/776"
Join key into the lexicon. Not a gloss by itself. Letters like a/b disambiguate homographs — AugIndex helps map them.
morphhb Morphology code Grammar as a compact string (OSHM)

Attribute morph. Example: HR/Ncfsa = Hebrew · preposition / noun common feminine singular absolute. Codes stack with / like the surface segments.

morph="HC/Vqw3ms" → conjunction + verb qal sequential imperfect 3ms …
Powerful for filtering (“show me all verbs”) · noisy on every ticket. Often drawer-only until you know you care.
morphhb Stable word id Immutable handle for this occurrence

e.g. 01xeN. Book-ish prefix + random-ish tail. Same word form elsewhere gets a different id.

Best key for your notes and invent entries on a specific occurrence — “this בְּרֵאשִׁית in Gen 1:1,” not every ראשׁית in the Tanakh.
morphhb Cantillation / hierarchy n Optional accents / phrase tree hints

Attribute n on some words (e.g. 1.0, 1.1.1) — cantillation hierarchy for phrase structure. Also separate accent marks already in the pointed text.

Advanced / optional. Great later for “breath groups” and poetic line feel. Easy to ignore at first.
morphhb Book · chapter · verse · order Where the token sits in the Tanakh

From OSIS structure (Gen.1.1) and word order in the verse. Remap scripts exist for English vs Hebrew versification.

Navigation spine of the study surface. Always needed; rarely “displayed as data.”
lexicon Strong headword (dictionary form) Citation spelling for H#### — not the verse form

From HebrewStrong.xml <w> inside each entry. The “fill choice” spelling in the dictionary.

verse may show וַ/יֹּ֥אמֶר · Strong H559 headword is more like אָמַר
This is the second version of the word you asked about. Drawer: “as written” vs “dictionary form” side by side.
lexicon Strong gloss · meaning · usage Short English from the Strong dictionary

meaning, def, usage, sometimes source. Tradition’s quick read — not your function bank.

Ledger lane / optional under-ticket. Never required for your invent entries.
lexicon Pronunciation / transliteration Latin-script crutches on Strong entries

Attributes like pron, xlit on lexicon <w>.

Nice for search and early reading. Easy to hide once Hebrew is the face.
lexicon BDB outline Deeper traditional lexicon structure (WIP in places)

BrownDriverBriggs.xml — longer, outline-ish. Linked via lexical index, not always complete.

Second-depth drawer after Strong feels thin. Not day-one surface chrome.
bridge AugIndex · LexicalIndex · TWOT# Maps MorphHB lemmas ↔ Strong ↔ BDB (TWOT for ref only)

Plumbing so b/7225 and 1254 a resolve cleanly. You rarely show this to yourself; the app uses it when opening the ledger.

Invisible infrastructure. Explore once so you trust the join; then forget it.
yours Not in the packages · your invent / function readings Letter combos Strong never named · program notes · becoming

Lives only in Concor / your store. Key by MorphHB id, by segment, or by free cluster you composed.

Primary intellectual product of the desk. The open data is fuel and foil — not the study itself.

What this suggests for “what goes on the surface”

You don’t have to decide forever. A sane default once you’ve felt the fields:

Always on the ticket: surface form (maybe show / segments).
Always yours: invent reading (may be empty).
One click away: lemma → Strong headword + gloss; morph expanded to words.
Later / filters: cantillation n, BDB, whole-Tanakh same-lemma gather.

If a field doesn’t make you want to move something with your hands, it doesn’t need a surface slot yet.
