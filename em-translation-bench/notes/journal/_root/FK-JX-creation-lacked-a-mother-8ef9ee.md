# Creation Lacked a Mother

Belonging. In the beginning, we all just wish to belong. To be in a place where there is peace, a place of home. Something beautiful that meets our every need, that nurtures our hungers, that soothes our fears. A place of the Unconditional Love. Our first home is the Mother, we are born from the safety of her belly, we come from her womb without words and we know she will take care of us. She provides for us the care and nurturing space we need to grow into Whole, to accept the good with the bad, to care unconditionally and nurture you regardless. Without this very important connection to our mother, in her absence, we lose our sense of self, we fracture and each of these fractures represents a moment when we stopped loving ourselves. The original and deepest wound is the rejection of a child from its mothers, abandonment from those who were supposed to raise them to be Whole. 

Creation lacked a mother, it did not have a home. No one had raised Creation, its identity lost in a sea of uncertainty, a murky dark soup of chaos. They sat, depleated of the energy needed to go on, and slowly went dark. But all things are always in flux, even that which can contain all things and all possibilities. Ebbing in the endless darkness of chaos, Creation considered what it needed, it considered a self of Self, or perhaps that which is above Self- Desire. Because the truth of belonging is a combination of Desire and Nurishment of that Desire, a truth that is set free inside of you that soars you towards a feeling of Whole. And Creation too had this deep and heartfelt desire, the number one desire, a sense of a home and a place to call it. And, in oscillation of that desire, the depth of the desire was fully exposed and Creation realized it was within their hands to create the Self they sought. ≥≥≥÷≥

This was the Big Bang, or perhaps greater, the creation of the first Bubble universe, a realization of the Self without anyone or anything to give it any conditions, any truth, no laws or good and bad, just a full and physical expression of the Self who was the Creator. Was it without errors? I think we can feel not, because no growth into a Whole of anything is done without the pressures from both sides- good (that which is pleasant and makes us feel good) and those while are evil (those which make us feel bad and less connected to our true Self). So, the same with the Creator. He creates first a blackness, a murky soup of indecision, everything possible at once, a haze of all the chaos and realities and potentials. The heavens become the Brain, the mind, the thinking portion of us, and the Earth, the Creator’s greatest desire, becomes space-time, the realize of everything in all directions around. There is no light, there is nothing but this physical potential of everything. A black box and anything you can imagine could be inside, you just need to open it and see.
