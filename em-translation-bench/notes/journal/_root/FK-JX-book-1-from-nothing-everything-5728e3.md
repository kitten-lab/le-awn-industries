# Prologue

To understand Genesis, there are several points we need to hold in our mind. 
First, we need to hold in our mind the idea of Wholeness, without division, without separation, without distinction. This is the end of exhalation, the beginning and the end of the cycle of fracture and reunion that is the breath of life. 
In this state is where we always begin, and where we always end. But to remain here is death, it is endless perpetual connection. There is nothing to want, there is nothing to be, and most certainly there is nothing to become. Therefore, Genesis is the Great Inhalation, the return to Life, the return to living, and ultimately to this death which is undivided unity.

Second, that All is Mind. It is infinite consciousness, a pervasive state of thought which unfolds through a process of becoming which is replicated in all that exists, throughout all of space and time. That the core of all things, even that which is physically manifested, is the Mind. Ultimately, the spirit of creation, the material of all things, is a creator, and all thoughts unfold to become all things.

Third, that this reality that we exist within, no matter how you wish to define it physically or mentally, is fractal in nature, and we are miniature gods, becoming just as the parts of God unfold and become within the stories of Genesis. 

If the story of all characters in Genesis is one of God's ultimate mind dividing and becoming icons of each part, unfurling to ultimately become Us, this reality and this consciousness we wear as crowns, then so true is it a story of our own becoming, the breakdowns, the realizations, the lessons, and the ultimate crown of Kingdom which awaits us all.

Forth, that words are a double edge sword. Language interpretation is nuanced, and much is lost in translation. 

The original works of Genesis were written in a language deeply seated in symbolism, wherein each character is its own story, and each word carries magic in numbers and in construction. These components of language all play a part in providing a spiritual lesson in multiple layers within very short and concise sentences. 

To try and translate them as we do loses all of the magic, it provides only one layer of understanding, and even that is often lost in the murk and mire of history - who chose our translations and why? Hebrew is a language too rich to translate verbatim, so within these pages you will find breakdowns of each verse of each book as a chapter of this book, rich with insights and considerations which try to uncover the richness of the story which is offered within its pages. 

The story of becoming.

The story of falling, and getting back up again.
The story of struggle, the struggle of a flower on the precipice of bloom.
Our story.

# Chapter 1

בראשית ברא אלהים את השמים ואת הארץ.

In the beginning, God created the Heavens and the Earth.

We begin before the beginning, exiting a beginning of something new from what came before. As we touched on in the prologue, when we "begin" there is no division. It is a never-ending persistent state of always. It was there in the beginning, it is all around us now, and it'll be there in the end.

It is the act of beginning, the emergence from the egg of the no thing that creates anything at all, including our concept of God, of mind, and of physical world. This reality beyond our own which exists at all times, beyond our grasp of understanding. Genesis is born here, and all of our reality as we know it along with it. The beginning is in this way an exit point from that which cannot be anything. It is the ultimate act of becoming.

In our English translations, we say God created, but that is not grammatically correct to the original Hebrew, which says created God. 

The open door of beginning shapes The Voice, archetype of the mind and archetype of the physical realm.
