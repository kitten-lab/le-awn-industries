In the beginning of the Heavens there was the Word and the Word was God and the Word itself was Thought. In the beginning of the Earth there was a bottomless abyss of waters and those were waters were a quantum field of all possibility. 

All is still. Lying dormant. For the moment, there appears to be no thing. No movement in the endlessly wide, endlessly deep pool, no observations in the sweeping breath of God, no desire, no thought. 

And suddenly - ! - an observation of Self. The thought of Something. It flexes and considers and pulls all that quantum water of potential into a singularity.  The first and smallest observation, the root of all which would be born from it. A point. A seed. A Yod. A dot of a meaningless thing alone in nothing. The seed which would become a tree that would bear all seeds.

Just a speck of physicality and yet the mind, the thoughts are always stepping ahead. What does this look like, to be a thing at all? How can I see me at all?
