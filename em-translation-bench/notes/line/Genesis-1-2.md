### translation
And the body which was coming was mark-less and had no in or out and this Nothingness drove the surface of the mark-less and the energy of **Theses** moved endless on the surface of the waters  (which are the potential material for all things)

### translation
The physical realm exists without end or beginning, without mark or distinction, desolate and empty. Ignorance rests upon and in the face of the end.
